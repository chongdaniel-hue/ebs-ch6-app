# Proposed Stylez store profile

Fictional classroom details for a Singapore clothes shop. These are suggested details, not verified facts from Suzzie's Gem. Use the same approved list in both the app and the Gem before students use the roleplay.

## About the shop
- Stylez is a casual clothes shop in a Singapore shopping mall.
- It sells clothes for everyday wear, school outings and weekends.
- Opening hours: 11 am to 9 pm daily.
- All prices below are in Singapore dollars and are the final prices customers pay.

## Products and prices
- Basic cotton T-shirt: $15.90 each; two for $28.
- Oversized T-shirt: $19.90 each.
- Polo shirt: $24.90 each.
- Short-sleeved shirt: $29.90 each.
- Denim shorts: $24.90 per pair.
- Jeans: $39.90 per pair.
- Casual skirt: $29.90 each.
- Casual dress: $39.90 each.
- Light jacket: $49.90 each.
- Canvas tote bag: $9.90 each.

## Product information
- Clothes usually come in sizes XS to XXL. Some items may have fewer sizes.
- Common colours are black, white, navy and beige. Colours depend on the item.
- Staff check the shelf and stockroom before confirming a size or colour is available.
- Cotton T-shirts are light and suitable for Singapore's warm weather.
- Customers should follow the washing label on each item.
- Wash dark colours separately. Do not use bleach unless the label says it is safe.

## Trying on clothes
- Customers may use the fitting rooms before buying.
- Up to four items are allowed in a fitting room at one time.
- Staff can help customers find another size or colour.

## Payment and promotions
- Payment methods: cash, NETS, PayNow, Visa and Mastercard.
- The two-for-$28 offer applies only to basic cotton T-shirts.
- Different sizes and colours may be mixed in that offer.
- Offers cannot be combined with other discounts.
- A receipt is given after payment.

## Exchanges for a different size or colour
- Bring the item back within 14 days of purchase, with the receipt.
- The item must be unworn, unwashed and have its original tags attached.
- An exchange depends on stock being available.
- If the new item costs more, the customer pays the difference.
- If it costs less, the difference is given as store credit.
- A change of mind does not qualify for a cash refund under this proposed store policy.
- Items marked “final sale” cannot be exchanged simply because the customer changes their mind.
- Faulty items are handled separately under the faulty-item process below.

## Faulty or wrong items
- Staff listen, apologise and check the item and proof of purchase.
- Examples include a broken zip, a torn seam or an item different from the one paid for.
- Staff refer the case to the supervisor to arrange a suitable replacement, repair or refund.
- If the item is damaged or faulty, staff must not reject the complaint just because it was bought during a sale.
- The supervisor handles requests outside the staff member's authority.
- These are roleplay procedures, not a statement of legal rights or a legal time limit.

## Refunds approved by the supervisor
- Refunds go back through the original payment method where possible.
- Cash refunds are handled at the counter after approval.
- Card refunds may take five to ten working days, depending on the bank.
- Staff give customers the refund receipt or reference number.

## Stock and reservations
- Staff check stock before promising an item is available.
- Available items may be reserved until the shop closes that day.
- There is no deposit for a same-day reservation.
- Stylez does not offer delivery or clothing alterations in this roleplay.

## When a customer is unhappy
- Listen without interrupting.
- Apologise for the trouble.
- Ask what happened and what help the customer needs.
- Explain the available options using simple words.
- Ask the supervisor for help if the customer is still unhappy or the request needs approval.
- Thank the customer for bringing the problem to the shop's attention.
